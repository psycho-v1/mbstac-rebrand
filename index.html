FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How is the MBSTAC utility token useful?

MBSTAC is not BDAG. It records uncompressed dashboard purchase and bonus as tokens. A wallet cannot mint more than that combined figure. MBSTAC does not currently have a monetary value.

How long will minting remain available?

Dashboard totals are not stored on-chain. MBSTAC exists to keep a permanent record of those two figures. Duration of the official mint window is set by the operator of mbasestack.com.

How can the contract be reviewed?

Copy the contract address from the official site and inspect the verified source, or ask an independent reviewer what the contract can and cannot do.

Can the procedure be tested first?

On the official site, yes: mint a small custom amount before the remainder. Do not use this repository for that test.
